PICNIC CLUB - Short Cake

Picnic Club Collection

Short Cake

Rs. 7,900

Product code: PC-SC-001

Description

Add a playful touch to your everyday wardrobe with the Short Cake outfit from our Picnic Club collection. Designed for a comfortable and stylish look, it is perfect for casual outings and everyday wear.

The set is inspired by sweet picnic afternoons. A soft white shirt carries delicate strawberry, heart and bow embroidery along the neckline, cuffs and hem, and it is paired with relaxed pink shalwar trousers that finish the look with a gentle, pastel colour story.

Fabric and fit

The shirt is cut from a lightweight cotton blend that stays soft on the skin and breathes well in warm weather. The straight silhouette with a slightly squared neckline sits comfortably without clinging, and the wide sleeves are finished with embroidered cuffs. The shalwar has a flared leg and an elasticated waistband, so it is easy to wear for a full day.

Styling ideas

Wear it with a plain dupatta and flat sandals for a college day or a family lunch. For an evening outing, add small pearl earrings and a light shawl. The colours also work well with white sneakers if you want a more casual, modern look.

Care instructions

Machine wash cold with similar colours and use a mild detergent. Turn the shirt inside out so that the embroidery stays neat, and do not use bleach. Dry in the shade and iron on a low setting from the reverse side.

Delivery and returns

Orders are usually dispatched within two to three working days and delivered across Pakistan within five to seven working days. Unstitched alterations are not available, but unworn items with the original tags can be exchanged within the return window shown in the Returns & Exchanges page.
